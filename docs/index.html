Access Policy Compliance Checker

Can an AI reliably follow written rules instead of guessing — and know when to punt to a human?
A written IAM access policy (5 roles, 6 departments, 10 named systems), a rules engine that encodes it, and 105 test scenarios checking whether an LLM applies the policy the way a human reviewer would — including 15 adversarial cases designed to test whether it recognizes genuine ambiguity, resists distraction, and knows when to escalate instead of guessing. Try the simulator below, or scroll down for the eval results. Full write-up on GitHub.
